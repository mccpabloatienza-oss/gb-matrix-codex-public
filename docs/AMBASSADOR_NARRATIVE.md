# Candidate narrative

## The Business Expert, Unlocked

For more than 25 years I have worked in marketing and business strategy, where the difficult part is often not collecting data but turning commercial questions into a useful decision framework. I used ChatGPT Plus to build the first Matriz GB from that domain knowledge.

Codex became relevant when the prototype needed a different kind of work: modularization, reproducible synthetic data, validation, automated tests, documentation and a GitHub-based maintenance workflow. This repository is deliberately independent from the commercial tool. It demonstrates the engineering process without exposing its data or proprietary formulas.

The evidence is inspectable: a seeded generator, pure indicator functions, executable tests, a browser demo, validation messages and a security gate. The human remains responsible for the business question, confidentiality boundaries and approval to publish. Codex contributes implementation and verification within those constraints.

## Why is this project relevant to the OpenAI Codex Ambassadors Content Path?

It shows a concrete transition from domain expertise to maintainable technical work. The result is not a claim that AI replaces engineering judgment: it is a reproducible example of a business expert directing Codex with constraints, reviewing failures, validating outcomes and preserving the distinction between original human work and later AI-assisted maintenance.
